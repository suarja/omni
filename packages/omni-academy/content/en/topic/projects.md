A project is a registered local root, not just a name in a list. Its observed inventory gives Omni evidence for assignments, drift, and capture.

The skill picker names the destination project and lets you choose the agents before adding skills.

[Add a plugin to a project](academy://tutorial/add-a-plugin-to-a-project) shows the safe path.

## In Omni

Workspace and project skills

Open a project node in `Workspace`, then `Manage skills`. Its tabs are `Skills`, `Instructions`, and `Info`. Use `＋ Add skills` for marketplace skills and `Refresh` to reread local evidence.

[Add a plugin to a project](academy://tutorial/add-a-plugin-to-a-project) follows the skill picker.
