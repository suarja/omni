A skill is a focused instruction set, often a `SKILL.md` plus references or scripts. It describes a way of working rather than a one-off answer.

Omni observes skills from marketplaces and projects, preserves their paths and package provenance, and treats changes as reviewable operations.

[Capture a skill from a project](academy://tutorial/capture-a-skill-from-a-project) follows the source.

## In Omni

Project skills and their content

In a project, choose `Manage skills`, then open a skill name on `Skills`. The skill sheet shows `Content`, its origin, and `Add to my marketplace` for an active project-only skill.

[Capture a skill from a project](academy://tutorial/capture-a-skill-from-a-project) follows that action.
