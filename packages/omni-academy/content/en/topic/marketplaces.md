A marketplace is a directory of packages with a known layout. Your personal marketplace is the central source from which projects receive assignments.

Omni reads the root, records availability, and keeps ownership and provenance visible.

[Connect your marketplace](academy://tutorial/connect-your-marketplace) starts the safe connection.

## In Omni

Workspace and Add Marketplace

In `Workspace`, choose `＋ Add`, then `Marketplace`. The sheet distinguishes `External source` from a first personal marketplace. Open a registered marketplace node for its observed resources; read the personal path in `Settings`.

[Connect your marketplace](academy://tutorial/connect-your-marketplace) follows the Add panel.
