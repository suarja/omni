A plugin groups related skills, commands, hooks, or manifests. It gives a team a meaningful boundary for inspection and assignment.

Omni groups a plugin’s observed skills in its marketplace canvas.

[Add a plugin to a project](academy://tutorial/add-a-plugin-to-a-project) walks through the skill selection.

## In Omni

Marketplace groups and project skills

The marketplace canvas groups skills by plugin. `Plugins and storefronts` opens plugin descriptions and actions. In a project, `Manage skills` then `＋ Add skills` selects individual skills, not a whole plugin with commands or hooks.

[Add a plugin to a project](academy://tutorial/add-a-plugin-to-a-project) follows those actions.
