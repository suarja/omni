The Activity sheet holds completed operations. Its optional Agent activity section shows live sessions, processes and worktrees.

Omni uses real local process and transcript evidence, not sample counts. Pending requests appear in Review; completed operations appear in Activity with their results.

## In Omni

Activity and Review

`Activity` holds operation history and, when enabled, the `Agent activity` section for live sessions and worktrees. `Review` holds pending requests and checks. Completed operations open their own result sheets.

[Install Omni for your agents](academy://tutorial/install-omni-for-your-agents) explains how a local harness becomes visible.
