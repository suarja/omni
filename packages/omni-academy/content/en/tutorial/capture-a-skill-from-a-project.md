## 1. Open the project’s skills

From `Workspace`, open the project, then `Manage skills`. Stay on `Skills` and find an active skill marked `Only in this project`.

You need a registered personal marketplace and a readable installed copy. An external skill uses the separate `Copy into my marketplace` action.

What you will see: the project’s skills, including a skill that exists only in this project.

[screenshot:capture-skill-project]

## 2. Read the skill

Choose the skill name to open its sheet. Read `What this skill does`, `Comes from`, `Installed in`, and `Content`.

For an active project-only skill, choose `Add to my marketplace`. This opens the capture sheet without writing the skill.

What you will see: the skill sheet with its content and `Add to my marketplace`.

[screenshot:capture-skill-detail]

## 3. Choose a plugin and add

Under `Place in`, select an existing plugin, or `＋ New plugin` and fill in its name and English description. An existing skill with the same name blocks the capture.

Read `What changes`: the destination receives the new skill; the project copy becomes linked to your marketplace without changing its files. The Git line reflects the current commit setting.

The final `Add` writes the capture when its prepared plan is ready. After approval, open `Activity` for its receipt.

What you will see: the destination plugin choices, `What changes`, and the final `Add` button.

[screenshot:capture-skill-preview]
